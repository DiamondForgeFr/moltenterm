## What this version brings

Brokk, the foreman in the sidebar, now gathers notifications, downloads, keep-awake and plan usage in one place that opens from his face, and you can talk to him there. Downloads from web pages now appear in the app, with progress, history, open and retry.

### Brokk and the sidebar

- Hovering Brokk's face opens his group: a rail of icons for notifications, downloads, keep-awake, agents and sessions, plus one panel. A click pins it open.
- When you write to Brokk, he answers in a conversation in place, with replies you can pick inline.
- His usage block shows a week grid for each agent and every provider, Codex included. The reset day is marked and the colours follow your pace.
- At rest the collapsed sidebar stays out of sight. It peeks out when there is news, and a running download shows as a ring around his face.
- A notification he brings clears once you handle or visit it, or when you clear it yourself. His settings can show a visit on demand.

### Agents and workflows

- You can put a short note on an agent from its context menu in the sidebar. Agents are grouped by project under a named separator.
- Workspace dots ripple while an agent of that workspace works, and agents can bring a page into view without taking focus.
- /morph opens its own mod design tab, with the agent's terminal on the left and the guide on the right. You can revoke a mod's trust in Settings > Mods.
- Guided workflows such as pipeline setup remember the choices you changed. Settings shows what they learned, and you can reset it.
- The Integration panel sorts work by what you need to do. Its ticket and pull request numbers open the ticket and the pull request.

### Browser, updates and security

- Settings > About checks for updates, downloads, verifies and installs them, then restarts the app.
- A view that holds a single site shows the site's icon in the sidebar, and bookmarks show their site's icon too.
- Browser cookies are stored encrypted on disk, and you stay signed in. A local page opened from a terminal asks before it loads, and a site gets a client certificate only when you pick one.

Also fixed: Codex no longer offers models your sign-in cannot use, terminal text no longer draws tiny, sidebar agent lists no longer go stale or miss running sub-agents, and the sidebar can be switched to floating again.
