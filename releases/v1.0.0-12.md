## What this version brings

Brokk's brainstorm now opens on every build. You talk an idea through with him while it grows as a 3D graph, and once the idea is clear you can turn it into a MoltenTerm project in one click. The web page panel also lets you draw zones on a version of your app and send them, with your comments, to its agent.

### Brainstorming with Brokk

- Pan and zoom the 3D graph, edit its plates yourself with Undo, and give the camera back to him with Follow Brokk.
- Each brainstorm has its own folder and conversation. You can load one, start a new idea, rename, forget or fork it, and unfinished ones come first.
- From a clear brainstorm, Brokk offers to create the project folder, set up SaaSFoundryAI and hand off to a coding agent, each after a preview.
- His talk window shows his answers as he speaks, in Markdown, and the field grows with your message. The first launch introduces him and /brainstorm.

### Web pages and the browser panel

- Draw zones on a version of your app and give each one a comment, reference images and links. They go only to the agents of the workspace and project.
- The version card shows where you are, what to test and the pull request state, and lets you switch or stop versions.
- Right-click a page to inspect it in docked developer tools, or to clear the site's data.
- The new panel chooser shows why a result matches and searches the web when nothing does. The search engine is now a setting, Brave Search by default.
- Agents reuse a page that is already open instead of opening it again.

### Agents, Integration and notifications

- Integration shows the work of the agents in its view and has a legend for its card colours. All projects shows each section once, with a project chip on each card.
- While a group merge runs, its suggested order is hidden and a second one cannot start. An agent that stopped without a pull request is shown with Resume.
- The activity indicator beside Brokk counts sub-agents and shows when an agent is waiting for you. The sidebar's agent list keeps its order.
- Lock an agent's first name for a project, and the next agent there gets it back.
- Archive one notification or all of them, with undo, and go to a finished agent from its card. Brokk's usage grid lists your most used providers first.

Also in this version: Brokk's hello, reactions, badge and visits behave consistently, notifications no longer cover his face, and logs rotate while the app runs and can be cleaned from Settings.
