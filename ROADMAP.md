# Roadmap

MoltenTerm's roadmap is a sequence of phases, not a calendar. It has no dates: each phase validates the next one.

- The phases, and why they come in that order, are in the "Roadmap" section of [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md#roadmap).
  The same file holds the principles and the non-goals that decide what is in and what is out.
- What is being worked on for each version is on the repository's
  [milestones](https://github.com/DiamondForgeFr/moltenterm/milestones). A milestone lists the open and the closed
  issues of one version, and nothing there is a promise of a date.
- To suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/DiamondForgeFr/moltenterm/issues).
