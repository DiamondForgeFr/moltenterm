## Allowed without asking

- **Unmodified redistribution.** You may redistribute an unmodified official build, source or binary, under the name
  MoltenTerm and with its logo.
- **Saying what is true.** "Based on MoltenTerm", "a fork of MoltenTerm", "compatible with MoltenTerm", "a mod for
  MoltenTerm", "works in MoltenTerm". Use the name in plain text, not in your product's name or logo.
- **Community use.** Articles, reviews, videos, talks, tutorials, user groups, forum and chat spaces, screenshots,
  the name in a repository's description or topics. Say it is a community project when it could be mistaken for ours.
- **Mods.** A mod may be called "<name> for MoltenTerm". Its folder or `mod.json` id may use any name that does not
  claim to be MoltenTerm or to come from DiamondForge. The mod's license is yours: see [LICENSE-EXCEPTION](LICENSE-EXCEPTION).
- **Your own build for your own use**, including modified, that you do not distribute.

## Not allowed

- **Forks and modified versions you distribute must rename themselves** and remove the MoltenTerm logo and the other
  brand assets, the application icons included, before the first release. "Modified" means a change to the code or
  to the built-in assets, not a change to your own settings, mods or configuration. Keep the copyright and license
  notices ([NOTICE](NOTICE)); the files from Wave Terminal also keep Command Line Inc.'s notice.
- **Names that look like ours**: a product, company, domain, app-store listing, package, account or social handle
  named MoltenTerm, or close enough to confuse (MoltenTerm Pro, Molten-Term, MoltenTerminal...).
- **Implying endorsement or affiliation**: that DiamondForge made, tested, signed or supports your build, your mod
  or your service, or that your paid product is MoltenTerm's.
- **Selling MoltenTerm's name**: charging for a product that carries the name or the logo.
- Using the name or the logo in a way that is misleading, disparaging or unlawful.

Wave Terminal's name and logo belong to Command Line Inc. and are not covered by this page; MoltenTerm does not use
them as its branding. DiamondForge's other product names (SaaSFoundry...) are not licensed here either.

## Distributions and packagers

A Linux distribution or a package manager that patches MoltenTerm may keep the name for a build whose changes are
limited to what its packaging needs (paths, dependencies, build flags) and that stays faithful to the official
behaviour. For anything else, rename it or ask.

## Asking

If your use is not in the list, or you are not sure, open an issue at
<https://github.com/DiamondForgeFr/moltenterm/issues> before you ship, and say what you plan to do. We will answer in
the open unless you ask for a private channel.

This policy may change as the registration progresses; a change applies from its publication, and past uses that
followed the policy then in force stay allowed. It is not legal advice, and it does not change the licences above.
